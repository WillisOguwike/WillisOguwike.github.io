## Project 1

**Title:** [Data Jobs Dashboard](https://github.com/WillisOguwike/WillisOguwike.github.io/blob/main/Data%20Jobs%20dashboard.pbix)

**Tools Used:** Power BI (slicers, button, drill-through, tables, power query editor, implicit measure)

**Project Description:**

This project involved analyzing the global data employment market to identify macro hiring trends for 2024 and provide granular, role-specific insights. It is designed to offer a multi-layered view of the industry, moving from high-level market metrics to detailed job profiles. This dashboard allows stakeholders to evaluate salary benchmarks, geographical distribution, and specific job requirements. The dashboard includes the following features:

* Executive KPI Summary: Displays total market volume (479K jobs) and global median salaries ($113K/year).
* Market Trend Analysis: A line graph showing job posting fluctuations across 2024, identifying peak hiring seasons and market contractions.
* Role-Specific Drill-Through: A deep-dive interface (demonstrated for Data Analysts) providing specific median pay gauges for yearly ($90K) and hourly ($32.50) rates.
* Global Job Mapping: A geographic visualization ("Where Are Jobs Globally?") identifying hiring hotspots across North America, Europe, and Asia.
* Workplace & Benefit Metrics: Ring charts detailing the prevalence of Work From Home (10%), No Degree Mention (39%), and Health Insurance (15%) for specific roles.
* Platform & Contract Analysis: A bar chart identifying top job sources (e.g., LinkedIn) and a treemap breaking down employment types (e.g., 88% Full-time).

Additionally, the dashboard includes interactive features for:

* Navigation: A "Drill Through" button and a "Back" arrow to seamlessly move between the global overview and specific job titles.
* Role Selection: A dropdown slicer to filter the entire dataset by specific professions like Data Engineer or Scientist.


Key Findings

* Market Volatility: The global data job market experienced significant turbulence in 2024, with a high of 53K postings in January followed by a sharp decline to a yearly low of 14K in November.
* Data Analyst Profile: Despite the global median salary being $113K, the Data Analyst role sits lower at a median of $90K, highlighting a pay gap between general data roles and specialized engineering positions.
* The "Remote Work" Myth: For Data Analysts, only 10% of roles are explicitly categorized as Work From Home (WFH), suggesting a strong return-to-office or hybrid trend in the current market.
* Lowering Barriers to Entry: Approximately 39% of Data Analyst postings do not mention a degree requirement, indicating a shift toward skills-based hiring over traditional academic credentials.
* Platform Dominance: LinkedIn is the undisputed leader for data job discovery, hosting significantly more listings than competitors like BeBee, Indeed, or ZipRecruiter.
* Global Concentration: Hiring is heavily concentrated in the United States and Europe, though emerging clusters are visible in Southeast Asia and parts of Africa.
* Contract Stability: The vast majority of available roles (88%) are for Full-time positions, providing high job security compared to contract or internship opportunities.

------------------------------
This integrated dashboard serves as a crucial tool for HR departments and career strategists, providing the "big picture" of the industry while offering the specific data points needed to negotiate salaries and understand regional requirements.

## Project 2

**Title:** [Medical Insurance Performance Report](https://github.com/WillisOguwike/WillisOguwike.github.io/blob/main/medical_insurance_2026_kaggle.xlsx)

**Tools Used:** Microsoft Excel (pivot table, pivot chart, power query editor, slicers, conditional formating, text box).


**Project Description:**


This project involved analysing medical insurance data to identify trends and patterns in healthcare expenditure across various demographics and regions from 2021 to 2025. It is designed to provide a comprehensive overview of key insurance metrics. This dashboard allows stakeholders to easily monitor and analyze average charges across different populations, health categories, and time periods. The dashboard includes the following features:

* Average Charges by Region: A pie chart representing the distribution of insurance costs across the Northeast, Northwest, Southeast, and Southwest.
* Average Charges by Record Dates: A line graph tracking the fluctuation of average insurance costs over a five-year period.
* Average Charges by Age Group and Gender: A grouped bar chart comparing insurance costs between males and females across five distinct life stages.
* Average Charges by BMI Category and Insurance Tier: A complex bar chart breaking down costs based on weight categories (Underweight to Obese Class III) and specific insurance plan levels (Bronze to Silver).
* Average Charges by Insurance Tier and Smokers: A comparative bar chart highlighting the significant cost difference between smokers and non-smokers across different plan tiers.
* Total Number of High Risk by Age Group: A pie chart visualizing the percentage of high-risk individuals categorized by age range.

Additionally, the dashboard includes interactive slicers and a timeline for:

* Record Date (Timeline): Filter data to view performance for specific months or years.
* Region: Focus on specific geographical areas to analyze local cost trends.
* Children: Drill down into how the number of dependents (0–5) impacts insurance charges.
* Age Group: Filter the entire report to focus on specific demographic segments.



**Key Findings**

* Regional Cost Distribution: Insurance charges are relatively evenly distributed across regions, though the Southeast (28%) and Northeast (25%) represent the highest average charges.
* Impact of Lifestyle on Cost: Smoking status is a primary driver of high insurance charges, particularly within the Diamond and Platinum tiers, where smokers face drastically higher costs than non-smokers.
* Correlation Between BMI and Plan Tier: There is a visible trend where individuals in Obese Class II and III categories incur significantly higher charges, especially those enrolled in Bronze and Diamond plans.
* Age and Gender Trends: The Senior (56+) age group consistently incurs the highest average charges, with males typically seeing slightly higher costs in the older demographics compared to females.
* Historical Volatility: Average insurance charges saw a significant peak in 2022, followed by a sharp decline in 2024, before beginning to stabilize/rise again in 2025.
* High-Risk Demographics: The Adult (26-35) and Young Adult (18-25) segments represent the largest portions of the "High Risk" pool, accounting for 22% and 19% respectively.

------------------------------
This dashboard serves as a crucial tool for the insurance company’s management team, providing clear, actionable insights into risk factors and cost drivers that inform premium adjustments and strategic planning.

## Project 4

**Title:** [Mobile Phone Addiction Dashboard](https://github.com/WillisOguwike/WillisOguwike.github.io/blob/main/Smartphone%20Usage%20Addiction%20Analysis.pbix)

**Tools Used:** Power BI (slicers, button, drill-through, tables, power query editor, implicit measure)

**Project Description:**


This project involved analyzing user behavior and psychological data related to smartphone usage to identify trends and patterns in digital dependency. It is designed to provide a comprehensive overview of how mobile interaction impacts daily productivity and mental well-being. This dashboard allows stakeholders to easily monitor and analyze the relationship between screen time, stress levels, and addiction across different demographics. The dashboard includes the following features:

* KPI Overview: High-level metrics for average daily screen time (7.50 hrs), work/study hours (3.24 hrs), and daily notifications (134.26).
* Work/Study Disruption: A donut chart showing the split between users who find phones disruptive versus those who do not.
* Addiction Prevalence: A breakdown of the total user base (approx. 7.5K) showing the percentage identified as "addicted" (29.23%).
* Stress & Addiction Correlation: A grouped bar chart visualizing how addiction severity (None to Severe) correlates with average daily screen time and stress levels (High, Medium, Low).
* Age-Based Screen Time: A line graph tracking screen time fluctuations across ages 15 to 35.
* Sleep vs. Notifications: A scatter plot analyzing the impact of notification frequency on average sleep duration.
* Addiction Segmentation: A treemap categorizing users into different addiction levels: Mild (32%), Moderate (38%), and Severe.

Additionally, the dashboard includes an interactive slicer for:

* Stress Level: Filter the entire dataset to focus on High, Medium, or Low stress categories.


Key Findings

* High Engagement vs. Low Productivity: Users average 7.5 hours of screen time daily, which is more than double the time spent on work or study (3.24 hours).
* Significant Work Disruption: Nearly half of all users (49.96%) report that phone usage directly disrupts their professional or academic responsibilities.
* Direct Link Between Screen Time and Stress: Data shows a clear trend where "Severe" addiction levels correspond with the highest daily screen time (approx. 9 hours) and reported "High" stress levels.
* Sleep Quality Impact: The scatter plot indicates a correlation between high daily notifications (peaking at 140+) and lower average sleep times, suggesting digital alerts interfere with rest.
* Addiction Demographics: Roughly 29% of the surveyed population is classified as addicted, with 38% of users already falling into the "Moderate" addiction category.
* Age-Related Volatility: Screen time is highly volatile between ages 15–35, with significant peaks occurring around ages 22, 26, and 35, suggesting specific life-stage triggers for increased usage.

------------------------------
This dashboard serves as a crucial tool for mental health professionals and researchers, providing clear, actionable insights into digital habits to drive wellness initiatives and healthy technology boundaries.

## Project 5

**Title:** [Amazon E-commerce Performance Dashboard](https://github.com/WillisOguwike/WillisOguwike.github.io/blob/main/amazon_sales_dataset.xlsx)

**Tools Used:** Microsoft Excel (pivot table, pivot chart, power query editor, slicers, conditional formating, text box)

**Project Description:**

This project involved analysing product data of Amazon ecommerce store to identify trends and patterns in sales performance for 2022 and 2023. This dashboard allows stakeholders to easily monitor and analyze the company’s performance across different regions, products, and time periods. The dashboard includes the following features:

* Number Of Reviews Across Star Ratings: Visual representation of star ratings (1-5) broken down by percentage.

* Total Revenue By Month: A monthly breakdown of the revenue generated, providing insights into sales trends over time.

* Quantity Of Product Sold By Discount Percent: Displays the quantity of categories of products sold by percentage discounts (0%-30%), allowing for easy comparison of quantity sold over product categories and discount.

* Revenue Generated By Regions And Product Categories: Highlights the total revenue generated from each region further brokendown by product categories, showcasing the performance in different markets.

* Quantity Of Product Sold By Regions And Product Categories: Highlights the quantity of product sold by each region further brokendown by product categories.

Additionally, the dashboard includes interactive slicers and timeline for:

* Month: Filter the data to view performance for a specific month or range of months.

* Product Category: Drill down into the performance of individual product category.

* Discount Percent: Filter the data by percentage discount.

* Region: Focus on the analysing regional performance.



**Key findings:**

* Regional Profitability: Identified the most profitable regions and highlighted regions where performance could be improved.

* Seasonal Trends: Revealed patterns in sales and revenue that correspond with seasonal events, allowing for more strategic planning.

* Top-Performing Products: Highlighted which product category are driving the most revenue, aiding in inventory and marketing decisions.

* Sales Volatility: Analyzed monthly sales fluctuations to understand market dynamics and adjust business strategies accordingly.
  
---------------------------------
This dashboard serves as a crucial tool for the Amazon ecommerce management team, providing clear, actionable insights that drive informed decision-making and strategic planning.
