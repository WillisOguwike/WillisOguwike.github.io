# Data Analytics Portfolio
## Project 1

**Title:** [Amazon E-commerce Performance Dashboard](https://github.com/WillisOguwike/WillisOguwike.github.io/blob/main/amazon_sales_dataset.xlsx)

**Tools Used:** Microsoft Excel (pivot table, pivot chart, power query editor, slicers, conditional formating, text box)

**Project Description:** This project involved analysing product data of Amazon ecommerce store to identify trends and patterns in sales performance for 2022 and 2023. This dashboard allows stakeholders to easily monitor and analyze the company’s performance across different regions, products, and time periods. The dashboard includes the following features:

Number Of Reviews Across Star Ratings: Visual representation of star ratings (1-5) broken down by percentage.

Total Revenue By Month: A monthly breakdown of the revenue generated, providing insights into sales trends over time.

Quantity Of Product Sold By Discount Percent: Displays the quantity of categories of products sold by percentage discounts (0%-30%), allowing for easy comparison of quantity sold over product categories and discount.

Revenue Generated By Regions And Product Categories: Highlights the total revenue generated from each region further brokendown by product categories, showcasing the performance in different markets.

Quantity Of Product Sold By Regions And Product Categories: Highlights the quantity of product sold by each region further brokendown by product categories.

Additionally, the dashboard includes interactive slicers and timeline for:

Month: Filter the data to view performance for a specific month or range of months.

Product Category: Drill down into the performance of individual product category.

Discount Percent: Filter the data by percentage discount.

Region: Focus on the analysing regional performance.

**Key findings:**

Regional Profitability: Identified the most profitable regions and highlighted regions where performance could be improved.

Seasonal Trends: Revealed patterns in sales and revenue that correspond with seasonal events, allowing for more strategic planning.

Top-Performing Products: Highlighted which product category are driving the most revenue, aiding in inventory and marketing decisions.

Sales Volatility: Analyzed monthly sales fluctuations to understand market dynamics and adjust business strategies accordingly.

This dashboard serves as a crucial tool for the Amazon ecommerce management team, providing clear, actionable insights that drive informed decision-making and strategic planning.
